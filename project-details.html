Pralay Shamak

Autonomous Firefighting and Rescue Robot - Innovating Safety Through Technology

←

Project Overview

Pralay Shamak is an autonomous firefighting and rescue robot developed by two innovative students to address the critical need for advanced fire response systems in India. The project was inspired by tragic fire incidents and has received national recognition for its groundbreaking technology and real-world applications.

Inspiration & Motivation

The project was inspired by two major fire tragedies in India that highlighted the urgent need for automated fire response systems:

  • Surat Takshashila Fire - A devastating incident that resulted in significant loss of life
  • Rajkot TRP Game Zone Fire - Another tragedy that demonstrated the limitations of manual firefighting in emergency situations

Technical Capabilities & Features

Primary Functions

🔍 Fire Detection

Advanced sensors and cameras detect flames and heat signatures automatically

🤖 Autonomous Movement

Navigates through buildings along designated paths without human intervention

🧯 Fire Suppression

Carries fire extinguishers and deploys nitrogen fire-suppression balls for larger fires

📍 GPS Emergency Alerts

Automatically sends GPS coordinates and triggers phone calls and SMS alerts

💨 Victim Rescue Support

Equipped with mini oxygen cylinders, masks, and first-aid kits

💡 Navigation Assistance

Lights and sirens guide trapped people toward safe exits

Advanced Systems

  • Communication System: GPS and GSM automatic alert system for emergency notifications during fire events
  • Real-time Monitoring: Camera surveillance system for continuous observation of fire zones
  • Mini Rescue Robot "Anuj": A secondary rover designed for confined spaces and air-quality monitoring
  • Victim Assistance Package: Oxygen masks, mini oxygen cylinders, and comprehensive first-aid kits
  • Safety Navigation: Advanced sirens and flashing lights to guide victims to exits
